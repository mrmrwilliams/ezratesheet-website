What Private Lender Rate Sheet Software Should Actually Do

A practical checklist: loan products, LTV/ARV logic, branded term sheets, deal workflow, and when CRM belongs in the same system.

MW
Mike Williams
Founder, EZ Rate Sheet · September 1, 2026

Search for “private lender rate sheet software” and you will get a mix of generic LOS tools, mortgage CRMs, and spreadsheet templates. Very little of it is built around how a hard money or private lending shop actually prices a deal.

This is a checklist of what that software should do — grounded in how EZ Rate Sheet is built, not in a fictional implementation. If a vendor cannot show these items in production, you are looking at a roadmap, not a product. See also the two questions we ask before signing anything.

The job to be done

Private lenders do not need another general-purpose CRM with a “lending” label. They need one place to:

  • Encode loan products the way the shop actually prices them
  • Generate a branded term sheet without re-keying numbers
  • See where every file sits from inquiry through approval
  • Keep every loan officer quoting the same rates

EZRS names that stack explicitly: custom loan products, branded term sheets, reporting and dashboards, and deal review and approval workflow. That is the rate sheet engine. CRM and marketing automation sit next to it (via RiseHub) when the shop is ready — they are not a prerequisite to price a deal.

What the pricing engine has to support

The live product supports Fix & Flip, Bridge, Ground-Up Construction, and DSCR. Each product is configurable with LTV/ARV bands, rate adjustments, fee structures, and state-by-state rules. That is the minimum. If your engine cannot hold those dimensions, originators will keep a side spreadsheet — and you are back to rate sheet vs spreadsheet.

A usable engine also has to survive messy deals, not demo deals: a one-off structure, a state overlay, a points change that should flow onto the term sheet without a developer ticket.

Term sheets, e-sign, and workflow

Pricing that never leaves the dashboard is not a rate sheet. Borrowers and brokers need a document with the lender’s branding, terms, and a signature path. EZRS generates branded term sheets and supports e-signature. The deal then has a home: review and approval workflow so the file is not hiding in an inbox.

The marketing site cites an average 15 hours per week saved and setup in under five minutes for EZRS Only. Treat those as the published product claims they are — not as a guarantee for your shop. Run the 5-day trial against your own products before you believe any of it.

If two loan officers cannot produce the same term sheet from the same file, you do not have a rate sheet. You have folklore.
