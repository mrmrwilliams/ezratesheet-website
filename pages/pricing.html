Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the free trial?
EZRS has a 5-day free trial on all tiers — no credit card required. RiseHub free trials vary by tier: 7 days for Lender Essentials and 14 days for Growth Engine and Full Stack. During your trial you get full access to all platform features at your tier so you can see exactly what you're getting before you commit.
What's the difference between EZRS and RiseHub?
EZRS is the rate sheet engine — it handles loan products, term sheets, deal management, reporting, and approval workflows. It's the core pricing and deal infrastructure. RiseHub is the CRM and marketing automation platform — it covers pipelines, unlimited contacts, email and SMS marketing, sales funnels, automation workflows, unified inbox, and more. Together they form the only combined private-lender-native rate sheet and CRM in the market.
Can I start with EZRS Only and upgrade later?
Yes. EZRS Only is a fully standalone product. You can use it independently as long as you want, and add RiseHub at any time by upgrading to Lender Essentials or higher. Your EZRS data carries over when you upgrade.
What does "DFY" mean?
Done For You. On the Full Stack tier, our team builds your borrower sales funnel, referral partner funnel, marketing automation workflows, and platform integrations — so you don't have to. You get a fully configured system, not just a platform login.
Are there long-term contracts?
No. Monthly licensing is month-to-month — cancel anytime. Setup fees and payment plans cover the one-time build and configuration, not an ongoing commitment. Once the setup is paid, your only recurring cost is the monthly licensing fee.
What if I need more than 25 deals/month but don't need Full Stack?
You can add Deal Packs to any tier — each pack is $25 and adds 10 additional deals to your monthly capacity. This lets you scale deal volume without upgrading your entire plan. If you consistently need 50+ deals per month, Full Stack's unlimited deal capacity typically makes more sense economically.
Do you offer custom integrations?
Yes. We can connect EZRS or RiseHub to your existing tools, loan origination systems, or internal databases. Contact us for a custom quote — pricing depends on complexity and scope of the integration.
