What's Inside RiseAgent

Nine capabilities, in plain English.

Seven are native platform features, productized for private lending. Two are EZRS-built, specifically for the deal itself.

RiseAgent Conversations

Auto-replies to inbound texts, emails, Facebook DMs, and Instagram DMs the moment a lead lands. Trained on your loan products, your overlays, and your tone.

Lender use: Broker DMs you on Instagram at 11pm. The AI replies with your program criteria and a single qualifying question. You’re first in line for the deal by 11:01.

RiseAgent Sales Agent

Handles the full inbound conversation across every channel — qualifies the borrower, collects deal info, and books the call. Unifies the thread when they ping you on three different channels.

Lender use: Saturday morning, broker emails AND texts about a fix-and-flip. The agent qualifies on both, merges the conversation, and books a Monday discovery call on your calendar.

RiseAgent Workflow

Build complex deal-stage automations in plain English. No flowchart drag-and-drop. No conditional logic to debug.

Lender use: Type “when a deal moves to underwriting, send the borrower the doc checklist and notify the loan officer” — and it’s built.

RiseAgent Content

Generate a week of branded social posts in five minutes. LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram. Stays in your voice.

Lender use: Stay top-of-mind with brokers and referral partners without writing a word. The market commentary writes itself every Sunday.

RiseAgent Email

Draft full borrower and partner nurture sequences from a single prompt. Edit, brand, and ship.

Lender use: “Write a 5-email nurture for fix-and-flip borrowers who didn’t close” — back in 30 seconds, ready to schedule.

RiseAgent Reviews

Respond to every Google and Facebook review in your voice, automatically. Local SEO improves itself.

Lender use: The five-star reviews you forget to respond to get a thoughtful reply within minutes. The one-stars get a calm, professional response that protects the brand.

RiseAgent Funnels & Sites

Spin up a geo-targeted landing page from a one-line description. “Hard money loans in Phoenix” — in 30 seconds.

Lender use: Test a new state, a new loan product, or a new broker channel without waiting on your developer. Spin it up, run ads, measure.

AI-Powered Borrower Support (EZRS-built)

Guides borrowers through deal entry inside EZRS. Answers basic loan questions, validates fields, and prevents bad applications from clogging the pipeline.

Lender use: Borrowers self-serve the application. Your team gets fully-formed deals, not half-filled forms with five back-and-forth emails.

AI-Powered Deal Analysis & Risk Assessment (EZRS-built)

Runs a risk read on every deal — LTV vs. comps, property type vs. program, borrower experience vs. project scope. Flags the deals worth a closer look.

Lender use: Triage 50 deals into 10 worth full underwriting. Reduce the surface area where bad deals slip through and good deals get missed.
