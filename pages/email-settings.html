Email Settings

Make sure our emails reach you.

Email providers can be aggressive about filtering — sometimes the messages you actually want end up in spam, promotions, or junk. Here's how to fix that in under 30 seconds, so it doesn't happen again.

Find your email client below and follow the steps for the device you're on.

Our emails come from:
sales@ezratesheet.com support@ezratesheet.com

Gmail

On the Gmail mobile app (iPhone or Android)

  1. Open the email from us.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Move to, then choose Primary.
  4. If asked whether to do this for future emails from this sender, say yes.

On Gmail in a web browser (desktop)

  1. Go back to your inbox.
  2. Find the email from us in the Promotions or Spam tab.
  3. Click and drag the email to the Primary tab at the top.
  4. Gmail will ask if you want to do this for all future emails from this sender — click Yes.

Belt and suspenders: Add sales@ezratesheet.com and support@ezratesheet.com to your Google Contacts. Gmail almost never filters mail from your contacts.

Apple Mail (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the email from us.
  2. Tap our email address at the top of the email (next to "From:").
  3. Tap Add to VIPs.
  4. If the email is in Junk, also tap the flag icon → Move to Inbox.

On Mac

  1. Open the email from us.
  2. Hover over our email address at the top, click the arrow that appears, and choose Add to VIPs.
  3. If the email is in Junk, click Message in the menu bar → Move to Inbox, then click Message → Mark → As Not Junk Mail.

Outlook / Microsoft 365

On Outlook.com or Office 365 (web)

  1. Open the email from us (check your Junk Email folder if it's not in your inbox).
  2. Click Not junk at the top of the email.
  3. Right-click the email → Security options → Mark as not junk.
  4. Then right-click again → Add sender to Safe senders list.

On Outlook desktop (Windows or Mac)

  1. Open the email from us.
  2. Right-click the email in the message list → Junk → Not Junk (or Mark as Not Junk).
  3. Then right-click again → Junk → Never Block Sender.

Yahoo Mail

On Yahoo Mail (web or mobile)

  1. Open the email from us (check your Spam folder if it's not in your inbox).
  2. Click or tap Not Spam at the top of the email.
  3. Open your Yahoo Contacts and add sales@ezratesheet.com and support@ezratesheet.com as new contacts.

Other email clients (ProtonMail, Fastmail, AOL, etc.)

  1. Open the email from us.
  2. Look for a button or menu option labeled Not Spam, Not Junk, Move to Inbox, or similar.
  3. Add sales@ezratesheet.com and support@ezratesheet.com to your address book or contacts list.
  4. If your client has a "safe senders," "allow list," or "whitelist" feature in its settings, add both of our addresses there too.

Still having trouble? Email support@ezratesheet.com and we'll help you sort it out.

That's it.

Thirty seconds of setup, and our emails will land where you actually see them. Thanks for taking the time.
