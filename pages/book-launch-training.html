Launch Training

Your system is ready. Let's train your team.

Your build is done and your account is set up. This is the call where we hand you the keys — a live walkthrough of your platform, so you and your team are confident before go-live.

  • What we'll cover. Logging in, creating rate sheets and term sheets, managing borrowers, and the day-to-day workflow your team will actually use.
  • Bring everyone who will use it. Loan officers, processors, anyone touching the platform — one training, full team.
  • Recording included. We send the session recording afterward so anyone who missed it (or needs a refresher) is covered.
