FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Capital Connector™ program?
HIS Capital's turnkey system for entering the private lending space. You get technology (EZRS), training, support, and access to capital — everything needed to start funding real estate deals.
Do I need lending experience?
No. The program includes step-by-step training, implementation guides, and ongoing coaching. Most Capital Connectors close their first deal within 30–60 days.
What is EZ Rate Sheet?
EZRS is the technology platform included in your Capital Connector package. It handles deal analysis, pricing, term sheet generation, referral partner management, and commission tracking.
How many deals can I do per month?
Unlimited. There's no cap on deal volume in your Capital Connector EZRS account.
Is there a monthly fee after the initial investment?
Your EZRS account and core platform access are included. Some optional add-ons or premium features may have separate pricing, which will be discussed during onboarding.
What if it doesn't work for me?
The program includes a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, contact the HIS Capital team for a full refund.
How quickly can I get started?
Immediately. You get instant access to all training materials and your EZRS technology suite upon purchase. Your onboarding Fast Start Call is typically scheduled within the first week.
