FAQ

Common Questions

What types of lenders is EZ Rate Sheet built for?

Private money lenders, hard money lenders, bridge lenders, DSCR lenders, and mortgage brokers focused on non-QM or business purpose loans. If you're pricing real estate deals manually, EZRS is built for you.

What loan types does the pricing engine support?

Fix & Flip, Bridge, Ground-Up Construction, and DSCR. Each loan product is fully configurable with your own LTV/ARV bands, rate adjustments, fee structures, and state-by-state rules.

How long does it take to get started?

Most lenders are up and running in under 5 minutes with EZRS Only. For plans that include CRM and automation (Lender Essentials and above), our team handles the setup and configuration for you.

Do I need GoHighLevel to use EZ Rate Sheet?

No. EZRS works as a standalone platform. GHL integration is available for lenders who already use GoHighLevel or want a full CRM and marketing automation system bundled in.

Can I integrate EZRS with my existing tech stack?

Yes. EZRS includes webhook endpoints, a REST API, and embeddable borrower survey forms. You can also connect through Zapier or Make without writing code. For hands-on help, we offer custom integration services.

What does the free trial include?

A 5-day free trial of the EZRS Only plan. No credit card required. You get full access to the rate sheet engine, deal workflow, and term sheet generation.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee for EZRS Only ($89/mo). Plans that include CRM and marketing automation have a one-time setup investment starting at $1,750, which covers full system configuration by our team.

How is my data protected?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security practices and never share your borrower or deal data with third parties.
